## Direction contract

THESIS: Pipeline health as an architectural, physically-layered command surface. Stacked glass panels at distinct depths establish at-a-glance priority — what needs attention now versus ambient context — refusing the flat single-plane card-grid every SaaS dashboard defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal-navy ground (near-black #0A0D13 deepening to #121623), three accent roles used with discipline and never mixed decoratively — electric blue (primary data/actions), cyan (positive/growth signal), violet (forecast/predictive layer). Glass panels: hairline borders at low-opacity white, backdrop blur, soft directional shadow implying real elevation rather than a flat drop-shadow. A crisp grotesk for UI and labels, tabular figures for every number. Depth comes from real CSS perspective/3D transforms on the pipeline funnel and KPI stack, not decoration pretending at depth.

STORY: A sales manager opens the dashboard and within one glance knows whether pipeline is healthy, which stage is leaking deals, and who needs a follow-up today. They scan the KPI row, read the funnel's conversion drop-off, click a stage to see its deals, open a deal for next-action detail, and check the forecast chart against target.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A flush charcoal top bar (logo, dashboard title, date-range selector, search, notifications, profile). Below it, a row of KPI cards as glass panels, each with a tabular-figure headline number, an accent-colored delta, and a small trend sparkline. Beneath the KPIs, the hero element: a full-width 3D-layered pipeline funnel — five stages receding in perspective with real glass depth, each stage showing deal count, total value, and conversion rate, hoverable and clickable. A subtle luminous node-network motif lives only as an ambient background layer behind the top bar/KPI zone, never behind data it would compete with.

FORM: Brief-pinned premium enterprise SaaS / command-center form. Not rolled through concept-seed: the brief specifies the material world (dark glass surfaces, blue/cyan/violet accent roles, layered 3D depth, restrained motion) in enough binding detail that running the dice would contradict "the brief wins." Executed at full commitment rather than the safe default rendition of this category: real CSS 3D perspective on the funnel rather than a flat progress bar skinned dark, a disciplined three-accent-role palette rather than one neon glow on near-black, and an authored SVG/canvas node-network hero rather than a stock photo or generic gradient blob.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
